COLOR WINDOW NUKE for Adobe Illustrator
=======================================

Illustrator pops the Color panel open whenever you click the Fill or Stroke swatches,
even after you closed it. Color Window Nuke stops that. You can still open the Color panel
yourself any time, with Window > Color or F6.


WHAT IT FIXES
- Clicking the Fill / Stroke swatches in the toolbar no longer opens the Color panel.
- The < key (Color) and the Appearance panel no longer open it either.
- If your Color panel is docked as a collapsed icon, its pop-out panel is hidden too.

Opening it yourself always works: Window > Color, F6 (or your shortcut), or a click on its icon.


INSTALL
1. Open each Illustrator version you use at least once, then quit it.
2. Double-click "Install Color Window Nuke.pkg" and follow the steps.
   This copy isn't signed by an Apple-registered developer yet, so macOS may say it
   "can't be opened". If it does:
     a. Click Done.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to "Install Color Window Nuke".
     d. Run the installer again.
   Your Mac's password is needed because the installer keeps a copy of the plug-in
   in /Library/Application Support.
3. Open Illustrator. That's it. There are no settings.


REMOVE
Double-click "Uninstall Color Window Nuke.pkg", then restart Illustrator.
Or delete ColorWindowNuke.aip yourself. In Finder choose Go > Go to Folder and paste:
  ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/
Then open your Illustrator's folder (for example "Adobe Illustrator 30" for Illustrator 2026),
then en_US (or your language), then Plug-ins.


GOOD TO KNOW
- The first Fill/Stroke click after opening Illustrator may show the Color panel for a
  split second while Illustrator builds it. Every click after that is clean.
- If the Color panel is docked as a collapsed icon, its icon stays highlighted after a
  hidden pop-out, and opening it then takes two clicks on the icon. Turning on
  Illustrator's Preferences > User Interface > Auto-Collapse Iconic Panels may help.
- If the Color panel is docked as an expanded tab (for example behind Color Guide), clicking
  Fill or Stroke still brings its tab forward. Illustrator offers no way to undo that.
- Tested with Illustrator 2026 (30.8.2) and Illustrator 2027 Beta (31.1) on Apple Silicon Macs.
  On other versions it stays inactive and Illustrator behaves normally.


PRIVACY
No internet access, no accounts, no tracking. It doesn't read your artwork or record what you
type. It writes nothing except the plug-in itself, a small list of where it was installed
(for the uninstaller), and the installer's copy.
