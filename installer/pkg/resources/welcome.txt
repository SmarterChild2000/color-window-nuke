Color Window Nuke for Adobe Illustrator

Stops Illustrator from popping open the Color panel when you click the Fill or Stroke swatches, press the < key, and similar actions. You can still open the Color panel yourself any time with Window > Color or F6.

This installs a small Illustrator plug-in into your own Illustrator Plug-ins folder, once for each Illustrator version on this Mac. Nothing in Illustrator itself is changed.

Before you continue:
- Open each Illustrator version you use at least once, so its Plug-ins folder exists.
- You can leave Illustrator open. The fix starts the next time you open Illustrator.
