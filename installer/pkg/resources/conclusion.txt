Color Window Nuke is installed.

Quit Illustrator (Cmd-Q) and open it again to start the fix.

The very first Fill or Stroke click after opening Illustrator may show the Color panel for a split second. Every click after that is clean.

To remove it later, run "Uninstall Color Window Nuke" from the download, or delete ColorWindowNuke.aip from your Illustrator Plug-ins folder (see the Read Me).
